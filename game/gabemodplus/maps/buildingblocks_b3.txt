This map was made with jbmod in mind get it here http://www.pcgamemods.com/8922/ also check HL2World for more updates on jbmod and Buildingblocks.

Just extract the map to your hl2/maps folder

then load up HL2 with jbmod and load the map thru the console by typing map buildingblocks_b3. The object of this map it to make things from the Blocks and other objects in the map and with the addition of the physgun you can make
boats and space ships with the spawnstatons.
